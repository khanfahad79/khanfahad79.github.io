A Tale of Two Cities by Charles Dickens is a historical novel set in London and Paris during the French Revolution. The story explores themes of love, sacrifice, courage, and social injustice. It follows characters such as Charles Darnay, Sydney Carton, and Lucie Manette as their lives become connected by the events of the Revolution. Sydney Carton is especially memorable because he sacrifices his life for Darnay, showing the power of love and selflessness.
